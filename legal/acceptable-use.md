## 1. Purpose

This Policy explains what you must not do on aijalon.trade. It applies to every user, creator and referrer.

## 2. Prohibited: access and eligibility

- Using the Platform from, or for someone in, a Restricted Jurisdiction, or while under sanctions.
- Using a VPN, proxy, Tor or any other means to hide your location or get around geo-restrictions.
- Creating more than one account, using someone else's account, or selling or transferring an account.
- Giving false information in attestations, KYC or payment details.

## 3. Prohibited: security and systems

- Probing, scanning or testing the Platform's vulnerabilities without our written permission. (A bug bounty with safe-harbour rules may be published later. Until then, report issues to [security@aijalon.trade] and do not exploit them.)
- Attempting to escape the strategy sandbox, access other users' data or keys, bypass MFA or step-up, or tamper with the ledger, signals or orders.
- Uploading malware, or code that behaves differently under review than in production.
- Denial-of-service attacks, excessive automated requests, or scraping beyond published API limits. Only use the read API as your plan allows.
- Reverse-engineering, decompiling or trying to extract or reconstruct strategy code or logic. This includes systematically collecting other subscribers' trades for that purpose.

## 4. Prohibited: financial misconduct

- **Money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions evasion,** or using proceeds of crime to fund your account or Fee Balance.
- Fraudulent payments; using cards or payment methods that are not yours; filing chargebacks for services you received (see the Refund Policy).
- **Market manipulation** through or in connection with the Platform: wash trading, spoofing, pushing prices or oracles, or coordinating with others to trade against subscribers.
- **Referral abuse:** self-referral, fake accounts, device or wallet farming, incentivised sign-ups that break these rules, or spam.

## 5. Prohibited: content and communications

This applies to reviews, posts, profiles, messages and referral promotion.

- Promising or guaranteeing returns, or making performance claims not based on Platform statistics.
- Giving personalised investment advice, or presenting yourself as licensed when you are not.
- Misleading, false or deceptive content; impersonating any person, creator, or us.
- **Sharing or reselling paid posts,** or content you do not have the right to share.
- Unlawful, defamatory, harassing, hateful, obscene or infringing content; personal data of others without their consent.
- Soliciting other users' seed phrases, private keys or credentials, or asking them to move funds off-platform.

## 6. Enforcement

We may investigate suspected breaches. Depending on how serious a breach is, we may do any of the following, without prior notice where the Terms allow:
- remove content;
- withhold rewards;
- move subscriptions to reduce-only or pause them;
- suspend or close accounts;
- delist strategies;
- claw back earnings;
- report to authorities;
- seek recovery of our losses.

## 7. Reporting

Report abuse to [abuse@aijalon.trade], and security issues to [security@aijalon.trade].
